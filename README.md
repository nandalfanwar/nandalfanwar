# About  

I build intelligent cyber-physical systems that integrate sensors, embedded devices, and software to solve real-world problems. My core passion lies in the embedded world and its practical applications—particularly how hardware and data can drive innovations in precision agriculture, UAV technologies, and emerging biomedical fields.

With a multidisciplinary background spanning Information Systems, full-stack development, and technical education, I enjoy exploring how technology intersects with different industries. Whether I am wiring up a new microcontroller, exploring autonomous aerial systems, or mentoring the next generation of coders, I am driven by the desire to create tangible, impactful solutions.

---

## Focus Areas  
- **Embedded Systems & AIoT:** Designing hardware solutions, sensor integration, and micro-soldering for real-world environments.
- **UAVs & Autonomous Systems:** Exploring Unmanned Aerial Vehicles and intelligent cyber-physical systems for mapping, monitoring, and practical deployment.
- **Precision Agriculture & Biomedicine:** Applying IoT and intelligent monitoring to optimize farming, livestock health, and medical applications.
- **Cross-Disciplinary Integration:** Bridging the gap between physical hardware, robust web backends, and data analysis to build complete end-to-end systems.
- **Technical Education:** Simplifying complex concepts and empowering others through coaching and mentoring.
